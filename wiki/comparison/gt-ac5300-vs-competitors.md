# GT-AC5300 vs Competitors (per PCMag 2018)

## Routers compared

| Router | Positioning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **ASUS ROG Rapture GT-AC5300** | subject of review | 8 ant, 8 LAN, MU-MIMO, IPS, WTFast, VPN Fusion, AiMesh, Alexa/IFTTT |

## PCMag's verdict (verbatim, summary)

> "Overkill for most home users, but... delivered the fastest 5 GHz close-proximity throughput... a must-have whether you're a serious online gamer or manage a high-traffic home network."

Editors' Choice (high-end routers, 2018) — 4.5 / 5.

## Notes / caveats

- Mid-2018 snapshot. Wifi 6 / 6E / 7 routers now dominate the category; PCMag's own leaderboard has since rotated. This page is preserved as a historical reference for the GT-AC5300 specifically, not as a current buying guide.
- Only D-Link, Netgear, and Linksys routers were directly tested against; the comparison column for the XR500 is mostly from contextual mention in the review (no full numeric table).
